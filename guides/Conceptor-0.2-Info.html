What Conceptor is

Conceptor is a small multitrack recorder for getting song ideas down quickly. It is a simplified version of the Honey Trap recording program.

It records up to 12 tracks, with amps, pedals and effects on every track, a drum grid, and mastering for the finished mix. It runs as an Android app and as a web app on iPhone, iPad, Mac, PC and Android.

Main features

  • 12 tracks. Four show at first. Add track shows more.
  • One stereo output. No grouping, no monitoring bus.
  • Eight effects on every track: Gate, Compressor, Pedal, Amp, EQ, Analog delay, Digital delay, Reverb.
  • Recordings stay clean. Effects are applied on playback, so they can be changed at any time.
  • Hear yourself through the effects while recording.
  • Import audio from MP3, WAV, M4A and other common files.
  • Gillian and Stephen: a 16-step drum grid with six generic drum sounds, tied to the click tempo.
  • Click track, tempo 40 to 240 bpm, loop.
  • Mastering: EQ, compressor and limiter on the whole mix.
  • Export the mix or one track. One track can be raw or with its effects.
  • Export as WAV or MP3 320k. Crop the start and end before exporting.
  • Undo last take. Songs save by themselves.

Limits in this version

  • 12 tracks. Each track holds up to 4 minutes.
  • One effect of each kind per track, always in the same order.
  • Drum grid: one bar of 16 steps, repeated.
  • Recording timing on the web app depends on the device. Use Recording delay to correct it.
  • The Android app file is on a private GitHub page. People without access need the file sent to them.
