Conceptor 0.2: User manual

Version 0.2 · 19 November 2026

About this manual

This manual covers Conceptor 0.2. The Android app and the web app have the same screens and buttons. Pictures are from the web app.

Differences between them are marked Android or Web.

  • Not in this version: Chrome extension, Mac and Windows installer programs, App Store app.

First start

  1. Open Conceptor. Tap Start.
  2. Tap Allow the microphone, then allow it in the box that appears. Or tap Carry on without it (no recording).
  3. Tap Next.
  4. Type a name for the first song. Tap Create the song.
  5. The Mix screen opens.

The Mix screen

From top to bottom:

  • Top bar: CONCEPTOR, the song name, and Menu.
  • MASTER: the volume of the whole mix, with level meters.
  • Drums, Mastering, Export: shortcuts. Drums and Mastering are gold when switched on.
  • Tracks: name, length, volume slider, level meter, and the buttons R, M, S, FX.
  • Add track: shows one more track, up to 12.
  • Bottom bar: time (position / song length), Rewind, Play/Stop, Record, Loop, Click.

Record a track

  1. Put on headphones.
  2. Press R on the track to record. It turns red.
  3. Press Rewind to start from the beginning, or leave the playhead where it is.
  4. Press the red Record button. The other tracks play. Play or sing.
  5. Press Play/Stop (or Record again) to stop.
  6. The track now shows its length. Press R again to disarm it.

Recording starts where the playhead is. Anything already on the track from that point is replaced. More than one track can be armed: they all record the same input.

Hear yourself

  • A track with R lit plays the microphone or input through its effects, even when stopped.
  • Use this to try amps and pedals before recording.
  • Use headphones, or the speaker sound feeds back into the microphone.

Play, stop, rewind

  • Play/Stop: starts and stops playback.
  • Rewind: back to the start, or to the loop start when Loop is on.
  • Playback stops at the end of the song, unless the click or the drums are on. Then it keeps going until Stop.

Undo last take

  1. Stop playback.
  2. Menu, Undo last take.

This puts back what was on the track before the last recording or the last import. Only the last one can be undone.

Add a track

  1. Scroll to the bottom of the track list.
  2. Press Add track.
  3. A new empty track appears. Up to 12 tracks.

Mixing

  • Track volume: the slider on each track. Far left is off.
  • Pan: on the track screen. Centre, or left and right.
  • Mute and Solo: M and S.
  • Master volume: the MASTER slider at the top.
  • Watch the meters. Keep the master meter out of the far right.

The track screen

  1. Press FX on a track, or tap its name.
  • Name: type a new name.
  • Input gain: how loud the recording comes in. Raise it if the meter barely moves while playing.
  • Volume and Pan: same as on the Mix screen.
  • Import audio: see Import audio.
  • Clear this track: removes the recording. The effect settings stay.
  • Effects: the eight effects, in the order the sound passes through them.
  • Back: returns to the Mix screen.

Use the effects

  1. On the track screen, scroll down to the effects.
  2. Switch an effect on with its switch. Its name turns gold.
  3. Tap the effect's name to open its controls.
  4. Choose a type, if it has types (for example Overdrive or Fuzz). Choosing a type switches the effect on.
  5. Move the sliders.
  6. Reset knobs puts that effect's sliders back to the starting values.
  7. Tap the name again to close it.

Order: Gate, Compressor, Pedal, Amp, EQ, Analog delay, Digital delay, Reverb.

Import audio

  1. Open the track (FX or its name).
  2. Press Import audio.
  3. Choose the file. MP3, WAV, M4A and most common formats work.
  4. Wait for Importing to finish.
  5. The file is on the track, starting at 0:00.

Import replaces what was on the track. Menu, Undo last take, puts it back. Files longer than 4 minutes are cut at 4 minutes. Stereo files become mono.

Gillian and Stephen: the drum grid

  1. Press Drums on the Mix screen (or Menu, Drum grid).
  2. Switch on Drums on.
  3. Tap a pattern under Start from a pattern, or start with Empty.
  4. Tap squares to switch steps on or off. Each row is one drum. Each square is a sixteenth note. Four squares make one beat.
  5. Press Play. A white outline shows the step playing.
  6. Set Drum volume.
  7. Press Back to return.
  • Drums: Kick, Snare, Closed hat, Open hat, Clap, Tom.
  • Patterns: Empty, Basic rock, Four on the floor, Half time, Shuffle feel.
  • The drums follow the tempo of the click. Change it with the Tempo button.
  • The drums are included when the mix is exported. With no recordings, the export is four bars of drums.

Mastering

  1. Press Mastering on the Mix screen (or Menu, Mastering).
  2. Switch on Mastering on.
  3. EQ: switch on and tap EQ to shape the whole mix.
  4. Compressor: on at the start (Smooth). Tap it to change it.
  5. Limiter: Loudness makes the mix louder. Ceiling is the highest level allowed (-1 dB is a safe setting).
  6. Limiting now shows how much the limiter is turning down peaks. Under 6 dB is a good guide.

Mastering works on the whole mix while playing and in the exported mix. It is not used when exporting one track.

Tempo, click and loop

  1. Menu, Tempo, click and loop.
  2. Tempo: 40 to 240 bpm. The drums follow it.
  3. Click track: switch on to hear the click. The click is not exported.

Loop:

  1. Play the song and stop where the loop should start. Press Start here.
  2. Play on and stop where it should end. Press End here.
  3. Press Loop is off to switch it on (or the Loop button at the bottom).
  4. Whole song clears the start and end.

Input, output and recording delay

  1. Menu, Input and output.
  2. Record from: choose Automatic, the built-in microphone, or a connected USB interface.
  3. Play through (Android): choose the speaker, headphones or a USB interface. Web: set this in the device settings.
  4. Recording delay: devices delay sound a little. Conceptor shifts new recordings to match. If a new part sounds late, move Adjust to the right. If early, to the left.
  5. Android: press Apply. Web: press Close.

Songs: new, open, save, delete

  • New song: Menu, New song, type a name, Create.
  • Open: Menu, Open a song, tap the name.
  • Delete: Menu, Open a song, Delete next to the name, then Delete.
  • Save: songs save by themselves after each recording and every few seconds. Menu, Save now saves at once.

Android: songs are kept inside the app. Web: songs are kept in the browser on that device. Clearing the browser's website data deletes them. Export to keep copies.

Export

Export makes a file. It asks one thing at a time.

  1. Press Export (or Menu, Export).
  2. What: The whole mix, or One track.
  3. One track only: choose the track, then With effects, volume and pan, or Raw recording, no effects.
  4. Format: WAV (full quality, large file) or MP3 320k (smaller file).
  5. Crop: move Start and End to cut the beginning or end. Use the whole length resets them.
  6. Press Export.
  7. Android: choose where to send or save the file (Drive, email, Files). Web: the file downloads, or on iPhone and iPad a share box opens; choose Save to Files.

When the end is not cropped, 3 seconds are added so reverb and delay fade out. A raw track export is mono and exactly the recording.

Limits

  • 12 tracks, 4 minutes each.
  • One stereo output.
  • Drum grid: one bar of 16 steps.
  • Undo: the last take or import only.
