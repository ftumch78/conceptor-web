Conceptor 0.4.3 works sideways (landscape). On a phone held upright it shows "Turn the phone sideways." Turn it and the app appears.

Not finished in this version:

  • Android 0.4.3. The Android app stays at 0.4.1 until its own update.
  • Signing in to a cloud account from inside Conceptor. The library still syncs through a folder that OneDrive or Google Drive keeps in sync.
  • The Windows and Mac apps are not signed, so Windows and macOS show a warning the first time. The steps below show what to press.

What is new in 0.4.3

  • Fixes: the screen keeps its place when a button is pressed (no more jumping to the top). The counter runs, in red, while recording. |<< stops and goes to 0:00. The Input and Output screen can be read. The Mac and Windows apps open filling the screen.
  • The whole app is sideways (landscape). The transport (|<<, Play, Record, Drop In, Loop, Click) sits in the top bar on every screen.
  • With Hand Hold on, point at any button (or press and hold it on a phone) to see its full name.
  • Tracking screen: every track's wave at the top (View All Waves, can be hidden), a row of shortcuts, then mixing-desk channel strips along the bottom: drums, the tracks, and the Master.
  • Each track has an In box: Input 1, Input 2, both (stereo) or None. Two tracks can record at once from different inputs.
  • Drop In: put the cursor where recording should start, press Drop In, pick the track and the run-up. The counter counts down in orange, then records in red until Stop.
  • Effects: 7-band EQ everywhere, knobs for everything else, and a pedalboard with nine pedals (Overdrive, Vintage Fuzz, Big Fuzz, Chorus, Phaser, Flanger, Tremolo, Wah, Compressor). Drag effects and pedals to change their order.
  • Wave button on each track: Trim, Insert Silence (paint over a part) and Clip Gain.
  • Mastering is now on the Master strip: EQ, Comp, Loud and Ceil (Polished Mode). The separate Mastering screen is gone.
  • Export: what, trim (with a cursor to listen from), mastering yes or no, format. It saves itself to Lt. Joe Bookman.
  • Guitar tuner in the top bar.
  • Lt. Joe Bookman (the library) and the Ultrasound Machine (the player) are their own screens, with playlists and a Drum samples folder.
  • Drum programmer: all 4 bars show at once. Each drum has Import, M (mute) and S (solo).

Recording one or two tracks

  1. Tap the In box on a track and pick the input: Input 1, Input 2, Inputs 1 + 2 (stereo) or None.
  2. Tap R on the track. It turns red. For two tracks at once, tap R on a second track with a different input.
  3. Press the red Record button in the top bar. The counter turns red and runs.
  4. Press Play (the square) to stop. Or press |<< to stop and go back to 0:00.

Two tracks is the most. Arming a third shows "Two tracks can record at once." If both use the same input, a message says so.

Drop In (re-record a part)

  1. Put the cursor where recording should start: tap or drag the ruler above the waves.
  2. Press Drop In in the top bar (the button with a red dot between two lines).
  3. Step 1: tap the track.
  4. Step 2: tap the run-up: 1, 2, 3, 5 or 8 seconds of playback before recording starts. 5 is the default; after that the last one used is marked.
  5. The counter shows REC IN 3, 2, 1 in orange, then turns red. Play along.
  6. Press Play / Stop (or Drop In again) to stop. The other armed tracks go back as they were.

Effects, pedals and EQ

  1. On a channel strip, press FX for the effects, or EQ for the 7-band EQ.
  2. The buttons along the top are the signal chain, left to right. Tap one to open it. Drag one sideways to move it; a gold line shows where it lands.
  3. Amp and pedals: all nine pedals are on the board. Tap a footswitch (the round silver button) to switch a pedal on; its light goes red. Drag a pedal by its name to move it.
  4. Turn a knob by dragging up or down. The value shows under it.
  5. EQ: drag a numbered point up or down. Double-tap a point to put it back to 0. Flat resets them all.
  6. Press Done to close.

Wave edit: Trim, Insert Silence, Clip Gain

  1. Press Wave on a track (or Trim or Insert Silence at the top of Edit Mode).
  2. Trim: drag the two gold handles. Press Apply. Everything outside them is removed; the rest stays where it was in the song.
  3. Insert Silence: paint over the part to silence with a finger or the mouse. Press Preview to hear it, then Apply.
  4. Clip Gain: turn the knob (-24 dB to +12 dB), then Apply.
  5. Undo goes back one step at a time. Clear forgets a change that has not been applied.

Export and Lt. Joe Bookman

  1. Press Export (on the Master strip, or in the Menu).
  2. Step 1: The whole mix, or One track.
  3. Step 2: drag the gold handles to trim the start and end. Tap the wave to put the cursor there, then Play from cursor to listen.
  4. Step 3 (Polished Mode only): with or without mastering.
  5. Step 4: WAV or MP3 320k.
  6. It is saved to Lt. Joe Bookman by itself. Then choose: Add to Playlist, Play in Ultrasound, View in Library, Share File, or Close.

Lt. Joe Bookman is in the Menu and in the shortcut row. Songs, Mixes, Tracks, Imported, Drum samples and Playlists are on the left. Files are deleted only here, and it always asks first.

The Ultrasound Machine plays anything in the library. Open a playlist to play it, re-order it with the arrows, or Take out a file (the file stays in the library).

Drums, tuner, Input and Output

  • Drums: all 4 bars show at once. Each drum row has Import (the folder icon), M and S. Import opens the library's Drum samples: press Listen, then Use. From device… adds a file of your own.
  • Tuner: the button with the little dial in the top bar. Play one string. Green is in tune, orange is close, red is out.
  • Input and Output: in the Menu and the shortcut row. One device per line, input meters for Input 1 and Input 2, and the recording delay with minus and plus buttons.

Android

The Android app stays at 0.4.1 for now. Nothing changes on Android phones in this update. Android 0.4.3 comes in a later update and will install over 0.4.1, keeping songs.

iPhone and iPad

Already on the home screen: open Conceptor while online. It updates itself. If it still has the old layout (a Master bar across the top), close it fully (swipe it away) and open it again. Hold it sideways.

New:

  1. Open Safari.
  2. Go to ftumch78.github.io/conceptor-web.
  3. Tap Open Conceptor.
  4. Tap Share (the square with an arrow).
  5. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  6. Open Conceptor from the home screen. Tap Start, then Allow for the microphone.

Switch off Silent mode, or there is no sound. Songs are kept on this iPhone or iPad. Use Export to keep copies.

Windows app

  1. On the computer, go to github.com/ftumch78/conceptor-web/releases/latest.
  2. Under Assets, click Conceptor-0.4.3-Windows-Setup.exe. It downloads.
  3. Open the downloaded file (click it at the bottom or top of the browser, or find it in Downloads).
  4. A blue box may say Windows protected your PC. Click More info.
  1. Click Run anyway.
  2. Conceptor installs and opens by itself. It has an icon on the desktop and in the Start menu.
  3. Allow the microphone if Windows asks.
  4. Choose the audio interface in the Menu, Input and Output.

Updating later: download the new Setup file and open it. It replaces the old version. Songs are kept.

Mac app

  1. On the Mac, go to github.com/ftumch78/conceptor-web/releases/latest.
  2. Under Assets, click Conceptor-0.4.3-Mac.dmg. It downloads.
  3. Open the downloaded file. A window opens.
  4. Drag Conceptor onto Applications.
  1. Open the Applications folder.
  2. The first time only: right-click (or Control-click) Conceptor, then click Open.
  1. A box says macOS cannot verify the developer. Click Open.
  2. From then on, open Conceptor normally (Dock, Launchpad or Applications).
  1. The first time you record, macOS asks about the microphone. Click OK.

Updating later: download the new .dmg, drag Conceptor onto Applications, and click Replace. Songs are kept.

Mac and PC in the browser

Already installed from Chrome or Edge: open it while online. It updates itself. If it still has the old layout, close the window and open it again.

New:

  1. Open Chrome or Edge (Safari also works on a Mac).
  2. Go to ftumch78.github.io/conceptor-web.
  3. Click Open Conceptor.
  4. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then Install.
  5. Click Start, then allow the microphone.

Library in OneDrive or Google Drive (computer)

Needs Chrome or Edge on a computer, and the OneDrive or Google Drive app on that computer.

  1. In Conceptor, open the Menu, then Lt. Joe Bookman.
  2. Press Library Location (bottom left).
  3. Choose Also in a synced folder (OneDrive or Google Drive).
  4. Press Choose folder. Pick the OneDrive or Google Drive folder. Allow Conceptor to use it.
  5. Press Sync Library, then A synced folder, then Start sync (or Combine and sync if a Library is already there).

Conceptor makes a folder called "Conceptor Library". Each song is a folder inside it, with song.txt, the track WAV files and the exported mixes. Nothing is deleted when syncing. If the same song was changed in two places, both copies are kept.
